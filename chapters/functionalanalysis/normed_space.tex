\chapter{Normed space}

Like how the metric is a generalization of distance, the normed space is a generalization of \emph{size}.

\begin{df}{Normed space}{}
  A pair \((E, \|\|)\) where \(\|\mathord{\cdot}\| : E \to \mathbb{R}\) is a normed space if \(\forall (v, w \in E \land a, b \in \mathbb{K})\),
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item \(\|v\| \geq 0\) (Positive definiteness)
    \item \(a\|v\| = a\|v\|\)
    \item \(\|v + w\| \leq \|v\| + \|w\|\)
  \end{enumerate}
\end{df}
