# Compact Calculus

This is a repository for a Calculus textbook, still very work in progress

## Goals and Vision

The textbook itself is separated into many books and parts and are laid out (as of now) as follows:

## Book I: Differential Equations
    Part I: The foundation of calculus: differential equations
1. Writing down nature: derivatives
2. Integrals and antiderivatives
3. Basic derivatives and antiderivatives
4. A study of graphs with calculus
5. Calculus, trigonometry, and oscillations (W.I.P.)
6. Merging calculus and dynamical systems (W.I.P.)
    Part II: Techniques (W.I.P.)
7. Basic techniques of integrations
8. Coordinate transformations
9. Advanced techniques of integrations
10. Approximation techniques
    Part III: Signals (W.I.P.)
    Part IV: Vector calculus (W.I.P.)
Book II: Real analysis
11. Invitation to real analysis
    Part V: Logic (W.I.P.)
12. Mathematical logic
13. Sets, functions, and relations
    Part VI: Number systems and their arithmetrization (W.I.P.)
14. The foundations: natural numbers
15. The integers
16. The rationals
17. Real numbers and sequences
    Part VII: Sequences (W.I.P.)
18. Sequences
19. Asymptotic behaviors: Landau's symbol
20. The theory of derivatives
21. Sequences of functions
    Part VIII: Series (W.I.P.)
22. Series
23. Darboux' theory of integration
24. A treatise on the abstraction of size: measures and Lebesgue's integration
Book III: Abstraction of the reals
    Part IX: Metrics
    Part X: Point set topology
Book IV: Merging linear algebra with calculus: functional analysis (W.I.P.)

The goal of this textbook is to be the textbook that's easy to read and concise, as the title says, "compact calculus".
The main focus of this textbook is to *deliver* the *intuition* behind calculus first, then build rigorousness later after the main ideas are fully developed. This motto is present in every chapter of the book.
